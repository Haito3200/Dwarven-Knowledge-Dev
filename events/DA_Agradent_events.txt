# Agradent: landing sites, pods and outposts. Written on 10 November 2026.
# The window is common/custom_gui/DA_Wonder_Agradent.txt; the numbers are in
# "Claude files/tools/setzunkhupa_2026-11-10/gen.py". Everything here needs DA_Agradent_travel_unlocked,
# which DA_AG_unlock_travel sets (the Ambassador's homecoming, DA_Dur_Vazhatun_event.67, or anything else).
#
#   .1          the road to Agradent: shown when travel is unlocked without the Ambassador
#   .11 - .17   hidden: a pod thrown from the window reaches its site, or does not (the dice)
#   .21 - .27   the landing at each site, with the first dealings there
#   .30         a pod is lost
#   .40 .41     the canal folk open the sealed archive (goodwill 85 with four outposts) / close the locks (below 15)
#   .50 - .52   every five years, with at least one outpost: a request for water, salvage rights, ring-fall
# Goodwill: variable DA_AG_goodwill on the country, changed only by DA_AG_goodwill_up / _down and the Southern Ice.
